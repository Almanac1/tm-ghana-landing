Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Transcendental Meditation Nigeria (“TM Nigeria”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit tmnigeria.com, submit your contact details, or register for an introductory talk.

On this page

  1. Information we collect
  2. How we use information
  3. Analytics and advertising
  4. Sharing, retention, and security
  5. Your choices and rights
  6. Contact us

1. Information TM Nigeria collects

Information you submit voluntarily

When you use our registration forms, we collect the information you choose to provide. The current forms ask for:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your country selection;
  • your telephone number;
  • your selected online introductory-session date.

Our registration system can also store a message if one is provided. We record the date and time when form and reservation records are created.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, analytics, hosting, and browser technologies may automatically process technical and interaction information. This may include your IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, visit times, and interactions with forms, links, and videos.

Campaign parameters contained in a page URL, including UTM parameters, may be available to Google Analytics for campaign attribution. TM Nigeria’s application database does not currently have dedicated fields that store UTM parameters.

2. How information is used

We use information where reasonably necessary to:

  • receive and process your introductory-talk registration;
  • contact you about the session you selected and send registration details or next steps;
  • notify the TM Nigeria administrator of a new registration;
  • respond to questions or requests;
  • operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the website;
  • understand how visitors find and use the website and its videos; and
  • measure the performance of campaigns and improve future communications.

Introductory-talk registrations and communications

The contact details you submit are used to process your registration and communicate about your selected introductory talk. The website sends the visitor a registration confirmation and sends the site administrator a registration notification. Registering does not require consent to unrelated marketing communications.

3. Analytics, advertising, and similar technologies

Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager

Our homepage currently uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand website traffic and interaction. GA4 may receive technical and usage information such as page URLs, referral and campaign information, device and browser details, approximate location, and interaction events.

The website sends events to a Google Tag Manager data layer for interactions that include reservation-form visibility and use, date selection, successful reservation, calls-to-action, scroll depth, FAQs, instructor content, social and outbound links, and video opening, playback, progress, completion, and closing. A successful reservation event may include the online session designation and selected date. An internal submission identifier is used in the page only to deduplicate that event, and session storage prevents it from being counted more than once in the same browser session.

Google Tag Manager is also installed to manage measurement tags. Tags configured remotely in the Tag Manager container can change independently of this website’s source code. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

LinkedIn advertising and the LinkedIn Insight Tag

TM Nigeria may use LinkedIn advertising services and may enable the LinkedIn Insight Tag. If enabled, these services can help measure conversions from LinkedIn campaigns, understand visits originating from LinkedIn, evaluate advertising performance, create or measure advertising audiences, and improve future campaigns.

LinkedIn may use cookies or similar technologies and may process technical and advertising information under its own privacy practices. At the date shown above, the TM Nigeria website source code does not contain a LinkedIn Insight Tag. This section describes the processing that may occur if the tag is enabled in the future, including through a tag-management service.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses or may use:

  • Django session and security cookies to support the two-step registration flow and protect form submissions;
  • Google Analytics cookies or identifiers to measure visits and usage;
  • Google Tag Manager to load and manage measurement technologies;
  • browser session storage to prevent duplicate reservation analytics events; and
  • YouTube technologies when you choose to open an embedded video.

GA4 and Google Tag Manager currently load automatically on the homepage. The website does not currently provide a consent-management control that blocks non-essential tags before they load. You can restrict cookies using your browser controls, although blocking necessary session or security cookies may affect the registration flow. Browser controls, privacy extensions, and Google’s available opt-out tools may also limit analytics.

4. When information may be shared

We may share or make information available only as reasonably necessary for the uses described in this policy, including with:

  • TM Nigeria personnel or administrators who handle registrations;
  • website hosting, database, and email service providers;
  • Google for analytics and tag-management services;
  • YouTube when you choose to load a video;
  • LinkedIn if its advertising services or Insight Tag are enabled; and
  • authorities, advisers, or other parties where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or establish or defend legal claims.

These third parties may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data retention

The website stores lead, reservation, and submission records in its database. We retain personal information for as long as reasonably needed for registration administration, communications, record-keeping, security, and applicable legal obligations. The codebase does not set an automatic deletion period. You may ask us to delete your information as described below, subject to any information we must retain for legitimate administrative or legal reasons.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the website and the information handled. However, no internet transmission, email system, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International data processing

Some service providers, including Google, YouTube, LinkedIn if enabled, hosting providers, and email providers, may process information in countries other than Nigeria. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from those in your country. Where applicable, we expect service providers to handle information according to their contractual obligations and applicable law.

5. Privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to request access to personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights may be subject to lawful exceptions.

Accessing, correcting, or deleting information

To make a privacy request, email us using the contact details below. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant registration. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Withdrawing communication consent

You may ask us to stop optional communications at any time by replying to a message or contacting us. We may still send messages that are necessary to complete or administer a registration you requested.

Children’s privacy

This website and its introductory-talk registration are not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so that we can review and address it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, our practices, or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised policy on this page and change the “Last updated” date above.
